Kamaleddin Lab · Toronto

AI for mental health that is testable, trusted, and translated.

We build and evaluate AI and neurotechnology that can withstand scientific scrutiny, support human judgment, and move responsibly toward real-world mental health care.

Explore our research Work with us
Questions driving the lab
Can agentic AI support clinical work without obscuring accountability?We design structured, auditable, human-supervised systems rather than general-purpose chatbots.
How do we know when an AI system is safe enough to trust?We study reliability, guardrails, failure modes, causal evidence, and clinically meaningful evaluation.
Can computation make brain interventions more personal?We connect neurophysiology, EEG, and individualized modeling to precision neuromodulation.

Research programs

One problem, three connected programs.

Mental-health AI becomes useful only when technical capability, clinical evidence, and human oversight develop together. Our programs are organized around that premise.

01

Human-governed clinical AI

Agentic systems for structured assessment, psychiatric training, psychotherapy evaluation, and decision support.

Trust, evaluation & causal evidence

NeuroAI & precision neuromodulation
