Research program · Toronto

Building AI that earns trust in mental health care.

I lead and contribute to projects in human-governed clinical AI, rigorous AI evaluation, and computational neurotechnology for mental health.

Explore research Collaborate
Questions guiding the work
Can agentic AI support clinical work without obscuring accountability?We design structured, auditable, human-supervised systems rather than general-purpose chatbots.
How do we know when an AI system is safe enough to trust?We study reliability, guardrails, failure modes, causal evidence, and clinically meaningful evaluation.
Can computation make brain interventions more personal?We connect neurophysiology, EEG, and individualized modeling to precision neuromodulation.
