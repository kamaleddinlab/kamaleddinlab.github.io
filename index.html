Research program

One question, multiple scales.

How can computational intelligence become genuinely useful in mental health? We work across biological, clinical, and population scales rather than treating them as separate problems.

Five connected pillars

Deep technical work, clinically meaningful questions.

Agentic & Generative AI

Multi-agent systems for structured psychiatric interviewing, psychotherapy training, simulated consultations, and clinical decision support with human oversight.

Responsible AI & Safety

Empirical evaluation of guardrails, sycophancy, personas, toxicity detection, digital twins, simulation fidelity, and identity-specific validity.

Population & Causal ML

Epidemiologic reasoning, interpretable machine learning, and causal methods for heterogeneity, treatment-seeking, suicidality, depression, and access to care.

Computational Neurotechnology

Neural signal processing, biomarker discovery, EEG automation, brain-state-dependent stimulation, deep brain stimulation and precision neuromodulation.

Neural Coding

Biophysical and computational models of excitability, spike initiation, multiplexed sensory coding, physiological noise, and degeneracy in neural systems.

Translation across scales

People & training

Research is a team sport.

Mentorship has spanned research staff, visiting PhD researchers, MSc students, and undergraduate trainees—many now working across academic research and technology.

Meet the network

Training philosophy

Trainees are encouraged to own a meaningful question, understand the assumptions behind the methods, write clearly, and build artifacts others can inspect. Projects can sit anywhere from clinical AI evaluation to causal inference or neural signal analysis.
