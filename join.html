Join / Collaborate

Work on questions that survive contact with reality.

We welcome inquiries from trainees and collaborators interested in clinically grounded AI, causal mental-health research, and neurotechnology.

Good fit

Curiosity first. Tools second.

Strong projects usually begin with a specific scientific or clinical question, not a desire to use a fashionable model. We value people who can move between implementation, evaluation, and domain reasoning.

Clinical & agentic AI

LLM systems, simulation, safety evaluation, human-computer interaction, clinical informatics.

Causal & population mental health

Epidemiology, causal inference, interpretable ML, large observational datasets.

NeuroAI & neurotechnology

EEG, signal processing, computational neuroscience, neuromodulation, brain modeling.

For prospective trainees

What to send.

A short, specific message is much more useful than a generic application.

01

Your question

Two or three sentences on the problem you want to study and why it matters.

02

Your evidence of fit

One project, paper, repository, course, or experience showing the skills you would bring.

03

Your constraints

Program, institution, availability, expected start date, and whether you need formal supervision or funding.

04

Your materials

Attach a concise academic résumé and, when useful, a transcript or link to code/writing.

For collaborators

Bring the domain problem early.

We are especially interested in collaborations where clinical or scientific expertise shapes the model from the beginning—rather than being added after a system has already been built.
