Collaborate

Interested in contributing or collaborating?

I welcome trainees and collaborators who value technical depth, clinical validity, careful evaluation, and human oversight.

For trainees

Projects span AI/ML, mental health, neurotechnology, statistics, and scientific software.

  • Strong coding or quantitative foundations
  • Interest in reading beyond your home discipline
  • Comfort documenting assumptions and limitations

When reaching out, include the question you want to work on, relevant skills, and availability.

For collaborators

I am especially interested in collaborations where computation can answer a clinically meaningful question or an AI system needs rigorous evaluation.

  • Clinical assessment and education
  • Foundation-model safety and evaluation
  • Population mental health and causal inference

Clinicians, methodologists, engineers, neuroscientists, and health-system researchers are especially welcome.
