Collaborate / Train

Bring a difficult question.

The best collaborations start with a problem worth solving—not with a model looking for an application. We are especially interested in questions where rigorous computation can change how mental health is measured, understood, taught, or treated.

Good fit

Where collaboration clicks.

Projects are a particularly strong fit when they have a clear human or clinical context and a meaningful evaluation problem.

✓Agentic or generative AI for psychiatric assessment, education, or workflow support
✓Safety, validation, calibration, and human-governed AI in high-stakes settings
✓Causal or interpretable machine learning for population mental health
✓EEG, biomarkers, brain stimulation, or computational neurotechnology
✓Research software that should be reproducible, reusable, and externally testable
Trainee inquiries

Make the first email useful.

01

Start with your research question

One or two sentences on the problem you care about are more useful than a generic statement about “AI in healthcare.”

02

Show the relevant background

Include your current program/role, technical experience, and links to a CV, GitHub, writing sample, or prior research where available.

03

Point to an overlap

Mention one research theme, paper, or software project from this site that connects to your interests and explain why.

04

Be concrete about the ask

State whether you are exploring collaboration, supervision, a research project, internship, thesis, or another training arrangement.

Potential project space

Questions worth exploring.

These are examples of directions consistent with the current program—not promises of funded openings.

Can clinical agents be stress-tested like medical devices?

Design task-specific safety and validity evaluation for agentic psychiatric systems under realistic variation.

When does a digital twin represent a person?

Move beyond aggregate model performance toward identity-specific validity, calibration, and longitudinal forecasting.

Can causal ML find actionable heterogeneity?

Use causal and interpretable methods to understand who benefits, who is missed, and why in mental-health populations.

How should AI-assisted EEG preprocessing be evaluated?

Combine automation with signal-quality benchmarks, expert review, and reproducible pipelines.

Which neural signatures actually guide stimulation?

Connect biomarker discovery with treatment response and individualized neuromodulation strategies.

What does human governance mean operationally?

Translate broad “human-in-the-loop” language into concrete supervision, escalation, audit, and accountability mechanisms.
