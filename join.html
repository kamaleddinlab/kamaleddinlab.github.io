We look for people who care about the question more than the buzzword.

The lab is a good fit for trainees and collaborators who want to combine technical depth with careful thinking about clinical validity, evidence, human oversight, and real-world use.

For trainees

Potential projects sit at the intersection of AI/ML, mental health, neurotechnology, statistics, and software engineering.

  • Strong coding or quantitative foundations
  • Interest in reading beyond your home discipline
  • Comfort documenting assumptions and limitations
  • Willingness to own a project, not just execute tasks

When reaching out, include a short note on the scientific question you want to work on, relevant skills, availability, and a brief background or portfolio if useful.

For collaborators

We are especially interested in collaborations where computational methods can answer a clinically meaningful question or where a new AI system needs rigorous evaluation.

  • Clinical assessment and education
  • Foundation-model safety and evaluation
  • Population mental health and causal inference
  • EEG, biomarkers, and neuromodulation

We welcome clinicians, methodologists, engineers, neuroscientists, health-system researchers, and industry partners with clearly scoped research questions.
