MAK
Lab lead

Mohammad Amin Kamaleddin, PhD

Research Scientist · AI & Mental Health · Computational Neuroscience

Mohammad Amin Kamaleddin leads a research program at the intersection of agentic AI, psychiatric epidemiology, causal and interpretable machine learning, and neurotechnology. The program is organized around a practical question: how can computational systems improve mental-health decisions while remaining valid, auditable, and human-governed?

His current research affiliations include the AI for Mental Health Program at St. Michael’s Hospital / Unity Health Toronto and T-CAIREM at the University of Toronto.

Training toward independence.

Projects are structured so trainees can move from contributing to a defined task toward owning a question, validating a result, and communicating it clearly.

1Understand the problem and literature
2Build a reproducible baseline
3Own an analysis or system component
4Stress-test and communicate the result
