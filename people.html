Lab culture

How we work together.

Trainees are encouraged to understand the scientific problem, challenge assumptions, and communicate across disciplines—not simply execute a technical task.

Question first

Projects begin with a scientific or clinical question and a clear claim that can be tested.

Cross-disciplinary by default

Clinical, engineering, statistical, and neuroscience perspectives shape projects from the start.

Reproducible by design

Code, prompts, evaluation choices, transformations, and limitations should be inspectable and reusable.

Research network

Built to collaborate.

Our projects connect researchers and clinicians across Toronto and beyond, including St. Michael's Hospital, the University of Toronto, Toronto Metropolitan University, York University, CAMH, and partner institutions.
