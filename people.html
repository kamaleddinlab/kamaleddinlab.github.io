Lab culture

How we work.

Question first

Projects begin with a scientific or clinical question and a clear claim that can be tested.

Cross-disciplinary by default

Clinical, engineering, statistical, and neuroscience perspectives shape projects from the start.

Reproducible by design

Code, prompts, evaluation choices, transformations, and limitations should be inspectable and reusable.
