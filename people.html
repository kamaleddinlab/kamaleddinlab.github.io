Working style

How I work with trainees and collaborators.

Projects are built around clear questions, transparent methods, and shared ownership of the work.

Question first

Start with a testable scientific or clinical question.

Cross-disciplinary by default

Bring clinical, engineering, statistical, and neuroscience perspectives together early.

Reproducible by design

Keep code, evaluation choices, and limitations inspectable and reusable.

Research network

Collaborative by design.

Projects connect colleagues across St. Michael's Hospital, the University of Toronto, CAMH, Toronto Metropolitan University, York University, and partner institutions.
