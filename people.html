Mentorship model

What trainees should expect.

Own a question

Projects are strongest when the trainee can explain the scientific or clinical question before explaining the model.

Build transparently

Reproducible code, explicit assumptions, careful evaluation, and clear documentation are treated as part of the research—not cleanup.

Communicate the why

Trainees practice writing, presenting, and translating technical work for collaborators outside their immediate discipline.

Future trainees

Interested in working at this intersection?

Potential projects can span agentic AI evaluation, clinical simulation, causal machine learning, digital twins, EEG, or computational neurotechnology. Inquiries should include your background, research interests, and one concrete question you would be excited to investigate.
