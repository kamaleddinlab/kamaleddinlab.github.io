Research

Three programs, one research problem.

How do we build computational systems for mental health that remain scientifically valid, clinically grounded, and useful across scales—from neural signals to population outcomes?

Shared spine

Every project passes through the same five questions.

What is the real problem? What assumptions does the model make? Where does it fail? Who remains accountable? And what outcome would count as useful translation?

01

Ground

Clinical, behavioral, population, or physiological context.

02

Model

Agents, causal models, simulations, or biomarkers.

03

Evaluate

Fidelity, safety, validity, bias, and robustness.

04

Govern

Human oversight, boundaries, and auditable decisions.

05

Translate

Training, assessment, service design, or intervention.

01
Human-governed clinical AI

Agents that participate in care without pretending to replace it.

This program develops and evaluates agentic and generative AI for psychiatric interviewing, psychotherapy training, clinical simulation, and decision support. The design goal is not maximum autonomy; it is useful division of labor between models and people.

Questions

  • How should multi-agent systems decompose psychiatric tasks?
  • Which parts of interviewing or training can be automated safely?
  • How should humans supervise, interrupt, and audit model behavior?

Methods

  • Multi-agent LLM orchestration and structured interviewing
  • Simulated clinical encounters and rubric-based assessment
  • Discrete-event simulation of service redesign
  • Human-in-the-loop evaluation

02
Safety, evidence & causal learning

Trust should be earned with evidence, not inferred from fluency.

This program studies when AI systems can be trusted in high-stakes, human-centered settings. We combine foundation-model stress testing with interpretable and causal learning on population data to distinguish useful signal from persuasive behavior.

Questions

  • How do guardrails, personas, and sycophancy change model behavior?
  • What makes a digital twin valid for a particular person or use?
  • Which social and clinical variables shape mental-health outcomes and access to care?

Methods

  • Foundation-model safety audits and behavioral stress tests
  • Identity-specific simulation validation
  • Interpretable machine learning and causal ML
  • Population and clinical epidemiology
Representative work

03
NeuroAI & precision neuromodulation

Use physiology to make intervention more personal.

This program connects computational neuroscience with clinically relevant measurement and modulation of brain activity—from EEG automation and biomarkers to deep brain stimulation, temporal interference, and foundational neural coding.

Questions

  • Which neural features predict response to intervention?
  • Can computational tools make EEG workflows more reproducible?
  • How do excitability, noise, and coding mechanisms constrain what we can measure and modulate?

Methods

  • EEG preprocessing and neural signal analysis
  • Biomarker discovery and oscillatory synchrony
  • Computational models of stimulation and neural coding
  • Mechanistic and translational neuroscience
